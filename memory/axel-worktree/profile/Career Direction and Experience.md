## Direction
- Building a serious career around energy delivery systems: the bottlenecks between generation and reliable delivery to people, businesses, and communities.
- Intended combination: mechanical engineering, energy infrastructure, safety, operations, project management, and practical AI tools.

## Strong Interests
- Natural gas and CNG infrastructure; gas distribution and transmission; energy access and last-mile delivery; utilities and mini-grids.
- Operations and maintenance, system reliability, safety and HSE, loss reduction, project delivery, and intelligent or AI-supported project management.
- Practical engineering solutions for African infrastructure challenges.
- Safety is a significant professional capability and possible long-term consultancy path.

## Experience and Community Work
- Sustainability, climate, and community-focused work has included Green Impact Project Assistant and Student Policy Maker roles at Cardiff University; sustainability and behaviour-change work; waste reduction and recycling initiatives; WORM Cycle circular-economy work; Sibol Ani hydroponics concept or pilot; Makoko water-filtration and community-resilience concept; climate and energy research; Think Big Challenge; youth representation; and community-centred engineering.
- Has participated in or explored mentoring, engineering societies, sustainability programmes, hackathons, research opportunities, internships, talent communities, Cardiff Rocket Lab, professional networking, technical writing, and article creation.

## Opportunity Filter
Prioritise opportunities that build real responsibility, technical learning, leadership, community impact, and professional credibility in energy, gas/CNG, utilities, infrastructure delivery, safety, operations, maintenance, engineering project management, research, sustainability, or technical consultancy. Do not recommend activities merely because they are available.
