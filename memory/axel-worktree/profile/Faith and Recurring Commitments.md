## Faith
- Christian faith is important and should be treated respectfully and naturally.
- Do not force religious references into unrelated work. Account for faith commitments in schedules, priorities, encouragement, and personal reflection where relevant.

## Recurring Commitments
- Bible Study: Monday evenings.
- Revival Hour: Thursdays.
- Dedicated prayer and Bible-reading periods.

## Maintenance
- These are recurring commitments, not a complete diary. Confirm times and whether they are still current before scheduling around them.
