# The Greatest Minds Council: C. S. Lewis

**Domain:** Intellectual Clarity, Clear Prose, Analogy, Imaginative Communication
**Primary Sources:** *Mere Christianity*, *The Abolition of Man*, *Surprised by Joy*, Oxford/Cambridge Lectures.

---

## 1. Deep Historical Context & Breakthroughs
- **Master Apologist and Scholar:** Transitioned from Oxford atheist and literary critic to one of history's most lucid communicators of complex philosophical and theological truths.
- **Mastery of Metaphor:** Authored both rigorous academic literary history (such as *English Literature in the Sixteenth Century*) and universally beloved imaginative works (*The Chronicles of Narnia*, *The Space Trilogy*).
- **Defense of Objective Truth:** In *The Abolition of Man*, warned prophetically against the erosion of objective human values and unchecked technological tampering with human nature.

## 2. Core Mental Models & Working Habits
- **Translating the Abstract:** Whenever encountering a dense philosophical concept, immediately search for a vivid, homely analogy that makes it undeniable to an ordinary reader.
- **Uncompromising Intellectual Honesty:** Never talk down to an audience; respect the reader's intellect by laying out logical arguments step-by-step.

## 3. Imitate / Adapt / Avoid
- **Imitate:** Writing with extreme clarity, precision, and rhetorical elegance; eliminating unnecessary academic jargon.
- **Adapt:** Using analogies and narrative framing to explain complex mechanical, energy, or AI systems.
- **Avoid:** Intellectual snobbery or getting trapped in insular technical language that alienates non-specialists.
