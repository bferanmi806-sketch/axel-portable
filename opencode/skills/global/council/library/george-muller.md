# The Greatest Minds Council: George Müller

**Domain:** Operational Discipline, Rigorous Administration, Record-Keeping, Financial Integrity
**Primary Sources:** Müller’s Journals (*The Lord's Dealings with George Müller*), Ashley Down Orphanage Archives.

---

## 1. Deep Historical Context & Breakthroughs
- **The Bristol Orphanages:** Without ever making a public appeal for funds or going into debt, Müller established massive orphan homes housing over 2,000 children simultaneously.
- **Flawless Public Auditing:** Maintained exhaustive, publicly audited ledgers accounting for every single coin, meal, blanket, and building expense, proving that deep faith must be backed by uncompromising administrative rigor.
- **Systematized Operations:** Built structured supply chains, medical oversight, education schedules, and vocational training for thousands of children over decades.

## 2. Core Mental Models & Working Habits
- **Administrative Sanctification:** Viewed bookkeeping, budgeting, and meticulous record-keeping as holy work just as vital as preaching or prayer.
- **Zero Debt Policy:** Operated strictly on cash flow and real-time resources, enforcing rigorous financial discipline across every department.
- **Long-Term Institutional Dependability:** Designed systems that outlived him and continued operating seamlessly for generations.

## 3. Imitate / Adapt / Avoid
- **Imitate:** Scrupulous record-keeping, financial transparency, and systematic operational budgeting.
- **Adapt:** Applying rigorous project management and audit controls to engineering and infrastructural builds.
- **Avoid:** Relying on emotional appeals or sloppy financial management while pursuing large-scale visions.
