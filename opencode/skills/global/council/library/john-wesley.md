# The Greatest Minds Council: John Wesley

**Domain:** Personal Routine, Time Stewardship, Habit Formation, Accountable Systems
**Primary Sources:** *The Journal of John Wesley*, *Rules of the Band Societies*.

---

## 1. Deep Historical Context & Breakthroughs
- **Architect of the Methodist Movement:** Traveled over 250,000 horseback miles, preached over 40,000 sermons, and wrote/published hundreds of books while maintaining a rigorous daily schedule.
- **Repeatable Human Systems:** Invented class meetings, bands, and circuits—small, highly structured accountability units that instilled discipline, moral support, and mutual growth among thousands of ordinary people.
- **Master of Time:** Scheduled every hour of the day with absolute precision, viewing wasted time as a direct failure of stewardship.

## 2. Core Mental Models & Working Habits
- **Minute-by-Minute Accountability:** Kept detailed personal journals reviewing his daily use of time, health, study, and interactions against strict performance benchmarks.
- **Systematized Discipleship:** Believed that spiritual formation and character do not happen by accident; they require methodical habits, early rising, regular fasting, and peer accountability.

## 3. Imitate / Adapt / Avoid
- **Imitate:** Protecting study and working blocks with strict routines; maintaining honest daily reviews of time usage.
- **Adapt:** Using small-group or AI-driven accountability check-ins to enforce project deadlines and personal discipline.
- **Avoid:** Falling into rigid, joyless legalism that crushes creativity and empathy.
