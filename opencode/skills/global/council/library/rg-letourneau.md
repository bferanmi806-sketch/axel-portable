# The Greatest Minds Council: R. G. LeTourneau

**Domain:** Mechanical Invention, Heavy Machinery, Industrial Execution, Faith-Driven Business
**Primary Sources:** LeTourneau University Archives, Industrial Patents, *Mover of Men and Mountains* (Autobiography).

---

## 1. Deep Historical Context & Breakthroughs
- **Revolutionizing Heavy Earthmoving:** LeTourneau transformed civil engineering and construction by inventing massive rubber-tired earthmoving scrapers, bulldozers, and electric-wheel drive systems that dwarfed conventional equipment.
- **Welding Pioneer:** He pioneered the widespread industrial use of electric arc welding in heavy machinery manufacturing, replacing heavy cast iron with lightweight, high-strength welded steel frames.
- **Institution Builder:** Founded LeTourneau University, seamlessly integrating technical mechanical education, business enterprise, and Christian service.

## 2. Core Mental Models & Working Habits
- **"God's Businessman":** Viewed profit not as an end in itself, but as working capital to fund global ministry, education, and heavy industrial development.
- **Shop-Floor Prototyping:** Spent his time shoulder-to-shoulder with welders and machinists, turning abstract mechanical concepts into iron and steel within hours.
- **Failure as Iteration:** When his early contracting businesses faced near-bankruptcy and heavy machinery broke under stress, he treated every failure as direct engineering feedback to strengthen the next design.

## 3. Imitate / Adapt / Avoid
- **Imitate:** Rapid, hands-on prototyping; maintaining unbreakable resilience when physical or financial projects collapse.
- **Adapt:** Applying industrial-scale thinking to heavy engineering, natural gas/CNG transmission, and infrastructure delivery.
- **Avoid:** Letting commercial success detach you from the shop floor or practical, boots-on-the-ground reality.
