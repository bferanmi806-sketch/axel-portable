# The Greatest Minds Council: Strive Masiyiwa

**Domain:** African Infrastructure, Telecommunications, AI/Digital Ecosystems, Regulatory Courage
**Primary Sources:** Stanford GSB Case Studies, *Faith Driven Investor* Interviews, Econet Strategy Papers.

---

## 1. Deep Historical Context & Breakthroughs
- **Breaking State Monopolies:** Fought a landmark five-year legal battle against the Zimbabwean government to break the state telecommunications monopoly, taking the case all to the Supreme Court and establishing the legal right for private mobile networks.
- **Pan-African Scaling:** Built Econet Global into a massive international technology, telecom, and renewable energy group operating across Africa and beyond.
- **Infrastructure & AI Vision:** Expanded into deep fiber-optic backbone networks, data centers, mobile money (Cassava), and renewable mini-grids, positioning African infrastructure for the global AI and digital boom.

## 2. Core Mental Models & Working Habits
- **Regulatory Courage:** Expect institutional resistance when building disruptive infrastructure; prepare for long legal and political battles with unyielding resolve.
- **Foundational Layer Investing:** Focus on heavy infrastructure layers (telecom towers, fiber cables, power grids, compute) upon which thousands of secondary businesses can thrive.
- **Uncompromising Ethics:** Walk away immediately from corrupt deals or political extortion, trusting that long-term integrity outperforms short-term compliance.

## 3. Imitate / Adapt / Avoid
- **Imitate:** Long-range strategic planning; standing firm against institutional or regulatory intimidation.
- **Adapt:** Applying systems thinking to natural gas/CNG distribution, utility grid bottlenecks, and digital tool integration in Africa.
- **Avoid:** Short-term pragmatism that compromises long-term institutional reputation.
