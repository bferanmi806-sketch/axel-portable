# The Greatest Minds Council: William Wilberforce

**Domain:** Long-Term Perseverance, Coalition-Building, Moral Courage, Strategic Patience
**Primary Sources:** Wilberforce’s Parliamentary Speeches (1789 onwards), *A Practical View of Christianity*, Parliamentary Journals.

---

## 1. Deep Historical Context & Breakthroughs
- **The Abolition Campaign:** Led a grueling 20-year parliamentary campaign against the British transatlantic slave trade, introducing annual motions, enduring repeated defeats, and facing immense economic and political opposition until the Slave Trade Act passed in 1807—and finally total abolition in 1833 just before his death.
- **Wider Moral Reform:** Simultaneously spearheaded campaigns for prison reform, education, working conditions, and the suppression of vice, proving that true moral conviction covers all dimensions of society.

## 2. Core Mental Models & Working Habits
- **Empirical Superiority:** Defeated emotional and economic arguments for slavery by burying Parliament under mountains of indisputable empirical data, shipping logs, medical records, and eyewitness testimony.
- **Patient Coalition-Building:** Forged strategic cross-party alliances, patiently educating politicians year after year until the cultural and political tide turned.

## 3. Imitate / Adapt / Avoid
- **Imitate:** Unyielding persistence when tackling systemic injustices or massive engineering/infrastructural challenges; grounding every claim in solid data.
- **Adapt:** Playing the long game in career, research, and nation-building rather than abandoning great visions after early setbacks.
- **Avoid:** Permitting discouragement or fatigue to cut short a vital mission before critical mass is reached.
